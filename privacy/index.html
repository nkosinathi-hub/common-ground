Common Ground privacy policy

Updated 10 November 2026. This notice explains how Common Ground handles your information.

Account and shared content

We store your sign-in email, profile and display name, space memberships, and the content you add, including events, watch history, notes, ideas, tasks, budgets, comments and files. Passwords, when used, are handled by Supabase Authentication as hashes. Google sign-in does not give the app your Google password.

Shared content is visible to current members of its space. Other members do not receive your sign-in email or Google authorization tokens.

Google sign-in and Calendar access

Google sign-in provides your Google identity, email address and profile information for your Common Ground account. Signing in alone does not grant Calendar access.

If you separately connect Google Calendar, the app accesses the list of your calendars and the events on calendars available to your Google account, including titles, descriptions, locations and dates. It uses that access only to display your calendars and carry out calendar actions you request. Creating, editing or deleting an event in Common Ground changes Google Calendar directly. Changes made in Google appear when the calendar is refreshed.

Personal Google events are not automatically copied into shared spaces. Google account permissions determine which calendars and events you can read or change.

Storage and sharing of Google data

The Calendar integration, when enabled, stores an encrypted Google refresh token on our Supabase backend so you do not have to approve access every time. Access tokens are handled on the server. Event data passes through that backend to display in your app; the integration does not persist copies of your Google events in the Common Ground database. It holds displayed events in page memory. Disconnecting deletes the stored grant and attempts to revoke Google access; you can also revoke it directly in your Google Account permissions.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. We do not share your Google events with other space members without an explicit sharing action.

Common Ground's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other services

Supabase hosts account and app data. Title searches go to TMDB through our server without your identity. Optional connected services such as Simkl process data needed for the features you choose. There are no advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline or disconnect Calendar access. Signing out clears app-owned device data; the server connection remains until disconnected, revoked, or your account is deleted.

Account and data provides account export and deletion controls. Google events remain in Google when you disconnect or delete your Common Ground account. Shared content you created may remain for other members, credited to a former member after account deletion.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the app owner using the support email shown on the Google consent screen. Do not post private calendar information in public issue reports.
