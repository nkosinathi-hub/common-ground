Common Ground

A private place to plan things with the people you invite.

Common Ground brings shared calendars, watch lists, notes, ideas, tasks and budgets together in spaces whose content is visible to their members.

Your Google account and calendar

You can sign in with Google. A separate Calendar connection, when enabled, asks for your permission to show and manage your Google events inside Common Ground. Each person controls their own connection. Personal Google events are not automatically shared with space members.

Calendar access lets the app list your calendars and read, create, update and delete events on calendars where your Google account has permission. Changes made here save to Google; refreshing the app's calendar loads the current events from Google.

You can decline Calendar access and keep using Common Ground's shared features.
